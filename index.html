01 / THE PROBLEM

WHY DRUG DISCOVERY IS DIFFICULT

Searching through massive chemical search spaces requires significant time, effort, and resources when relying solely on unassisted experimental screening.

Vast Search Space

With over 1060 possible drug-like molecules, testing candidates exhaustively in physical labs is impossible.

High Resource Burden

Physical synthesis and biological assays demand extensive time, specialized equipment, and manual lab effort.

Complex Multi-Properties

A candidate must balance target binding, solubility, toxicity, and bioavailability simultaneously.

02 / COMPUTATIONAL ASSISTANCE

HOW AI HELPS RESEARCHERS

Machine-learning models learn patterns from existing biological data to estimate properties of new compounds before lab synthesis.

Virtual Screening

Evaluates virtual libraries computationally to filter out unpromising structures early.

Lead Prioritization

Orders candidates so researchers focus physical experiments on high-confidence options first.

03 / REALITY CHECK

LIMITATIONS & SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY

AI predictions are computational hypotheses. They support decision-making but do not replace empirical proof.

01 Data Quality & Training Dependence
Models depend heavily on the quality, diversity, and relevance of their training datasets. Biased data leads to flawed property estimates.
02 Mandatory Experimental Validation
Predictions must be tested in physical biological assays, preclinical models, and clinical trials to confirm therapeutic activity and safety.
04 / SEMINAR MATERIALS

SEMINAR ABSTRACT & REFERENCES

Seminar Abstract

This presentation examines artificial intelligence applications in early-stage drug discovery. By converting molecular structures into numerical representations, machine learning models estimate binding activity and toxicity profiles to prioritize candidates. While computational screening streamlines search focus, biological validation in laboratory and clinical settings remains essential.

Academic References

  • Jiménez-Luna et al. (2021) Drug discovery with explainable AI. Nature Machine Intelligence.
