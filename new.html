The Problem

Why is drug discovery so difficult?

Drug discovery involves evaluating many possible candidates while balancing activity, molecular properties, safety, and other scientific requirements.

The overall development process can require extensive time, money, expertise, and experimental work.

These figures describe the broader drug discovery and development challenge — not the cost of screening a small candidate pool alone. See References.

AI can process complex biological and chemical data, identify patterns, estimate properties, and help researchers prioritize candidates for further investigation.

AI does not remove experiments.
It helps researchers decide where to investigate first.

Process

An AI-assisted drug discovery pipeline

AI tools can support many stages. Below is a simplified illustration of where AI commonly contributes to a discovery and development workflow.

  1. 01

    Target Identification

    Identify a biological target relevant to a disease.

  2. 02

    Virtual Screening

    Computationally evaluate candidate molecules against the target.

  3. 03

    Molecular Property Prediction

    Estimate solubility, activity, toxicity, and other key properties.

  4. 04

    Lead Optimization

    Refine promising candidates to improve desired properties.

  5. 05

    Preclinical Safety Assessment

    Support scientists assessing safety signals before human studies.

  6. 06

    Drug Development

    Move toward clinical and regulatory evaluation with experimental evidence.

Applications

Where AI contributes today

Target Identification

Finding biological targets associated with a disease using genomic, proteomic, and literature data.

Virtual Screening

Computationally evaluating large virtual libraries against a target to focus downstream work.

Molecular Property Prediction

Estimating activity, solubility, toxicity, and drug-like properties before laboratory testing.

Lead Optimization

Suggesting structural modifications to improve the profile of promising candidates.

Preclinical Safety

Assisting the assessment of safety indicators prior to clinical trials.

Benefits

Potential benefits of AI assistance

AI may help reduce time, cost, and effort by improving efficiency and focusing experimental work on more promising candidates.

More Focused Search

Investigate a smaller, higher-priority set of candidates.

Reduced Repetitive Analysis

Automate routine computational analysis of candidate properties.

Better Candidate Prioritization

Rank candidates using learned patterns from biological and chemical data.

Support for Research Decisions

Provide additional computational evidence to inform experimental plans.

We do not claim specific percentage savings. The exact impact depends on the disease, data, and research context.

If AI is powerful, why is drug discovery still hard?
